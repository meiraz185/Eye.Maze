About

Play. Look left, right, up or down and the light follows. Look at the middle and it rests. Find the green portal — three mazes.

Setup. None. After START, allow the camera and look at the ring for a moment. The gear icon in the game has optional sensitivity settings and a quick calibration.

Privacy. The camera image is processed by an on-device face model in this browser. Video is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Best with even light on your face and the webcam roughly at eye level.
